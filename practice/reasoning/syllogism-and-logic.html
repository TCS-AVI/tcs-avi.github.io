1. Statements: All pianos are drums. All flutes are drums. Conclusions: I. All pianos are flutes. II. All flutes are pianos.

  1. Only conclusion I follows
  2. Only conclusion II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows

Answer: D) Neither I nor II follows

A and C are both inside B but need not relate to each other.

20. A triangle is divided by 2 lines drawn from the top vertex to the base, cutting the base into 3 parts. How many triangles are there in total?
