1. A man starts from a point and walks 7 km towards West, turns right and walks 9 km, turns left and walks 5 km. How far is he from the starting point?

  1. 16 km
  2. 14 km
  3. 15 km
  4. 17 km

Answer: C) 15 km

Net displacement is 12 km and 9 km at right angles, so the distance is 15 km.

2. A boy starts from his school and walks 3 km towards East, turns left and walks 8 km, turns left and walks 7 km, turns left and walks 12 km. In which direction is he now with respect to the school?

  1. North-East
  2. South-West
  3. South
  4. West

Answer: B) South-West

Taking East as +x and North as +y, the net displacement is (-4, -4), which lies to the South-West.

22. One morning, after sunrise, Vikram stood facing a pole and his shadow fell to his right. Which direction was he facing?

  1. South
  2. North
  3. East
  4. West

Answer: B) North

In the morning the sun is in the east, so the shadow falls west. The shadow is on his right, so his right is west, and he faces north.

  1. 15
  2. 11
  3. 13
  4. 14

Straight-line distance = sqrt(12^2 + 5^2) = 13 km.
