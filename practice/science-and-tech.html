4. Which one of the following is NOT correct?

  1. Chlorophyll contains magnesium
  2. Haemoglobin contains iron
  3. Vitamin B12 contains cobalt
  4. Thyroxine contains calcium

Answer: D) Thyroxine contains calcium

Chlorophyll has magnesium at the centre of its porphyrin ring. Haemoglobin has iron in haem. Vitamin B12 (cyanocobalamin) contains cobalt. Thyroxine is an iodine-containing hormone of the thyroid gland, not calcium-containing, so the last statement is incorrect.

5. Assertion (A): The clear sky appears blue in colour. Reason (R): Blue light has a longer wavelength than red light and is therefore scattered more by air molecules. Choose the correct option.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: C) A is true but R is false

A is true: air molecules scatter sunlight and blue dominates what reaches our eyes. R is false: blue light has a shorter wavelength than red. Rayleigh scattering is inversely proportional to the fourth power of wavelength, so shorter blue wavelengths scatter more. R therefore wrongly states the wavelength relationship.

6. Which one of the following statements is NOT correct?

  1. The mass of a body remains the same on the Earth and on the Moon
  2. The weight of a body on the Moon is about one-sixth of its weight on the Earth
  3. The acceleration due to gravity has the same value at the equator and at the poles
  4. The acceleration due to gravity decreases with increasing height above the Earth's surface

Answer: C) The acceleration due to gravity has the same value at the equator and at the poles

Mass is intrinsic and does not change with location. Moon's gravity is about one-sixth of Earth's, so weight is about one-sixth. Earth is oblate and rotating, so g is slightly greater at the poles than at the equator; the statement of equality is incorrect. g also falls with altitude.

13. Match List I with List II. List I: A. Washing soda; B. Baking soda; C. Bleaching powder; D. Plaster of Paris List II: 1. CaOCl2; 2. Na2CO3.10H2O; 3. CaSO4.1/2H2O; 4. NaHCO3 Choose the correct combination.

  1. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  2. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
  3. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  4. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1

Answer: B) A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3

Washing soda is sodium carbonate decahydrate, Na2CO3.10H2O (A-2). Baking soda is sodium bicarbonate, NaHCO3 (B-4). Bleaching powder is calcium oxychloride, CaOCl2 (C-1). Plaster of Paris is calcium sulphate hemihydrate, CaSO4.1/2H2O (D-3). Hence A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3.
