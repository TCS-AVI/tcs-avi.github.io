1. Consider the following statements regarding the greetings extended by PM Narendra Modi: 1. Prime Minister Narendra Modi congratulated Bharat Ratna Shri Lal Krishna Advani. 2. Shri Lal Krishna Advani has entered the 100th year of his life. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: C) Both 1 and 2

Prime Minister Narendra Modi congratulated Bharat Ratna Shri Lal Krishna Advani on entering the 100th year of his life. Both statements are directly derived from the official news headline.

4. The Turing Award is given in which field?

  1. Physics
  2. Medicine
  3. Chemistry
  4. Computer science

Answer: D) Computer science

The Turing Award, given by the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM), is the top honour in computer science, named after Alan Turing. It is often called the Nobel of computing.

  1. Physics
  2. Medicine
  3. Chemistry
  4. Economics

6. The Fields Medal is awarded in which subject?

  1. Physics
  2. Economics
  3. Mathematics
  4. Computer science

Answer: C) Mathematics

The Fields Medal is awarded in mathematics, to mathematicians under 40, at the International Congress of Mathematicians held every four years. It is often called the Nobel of mathematics.

8. The Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize is awarded for which field?

  1. Literature
  2. Peace
  3. Science and technology
  4. Arts

Answer: C) Science and technology

The Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize is India's major award for science and technology, given by CSIR for outstanding research. It is named after CSIR's founder director, S. S. Bhatnagar. It was first awarded in 1958.

9. C. V. Raman won the Nobel Prize in which field?

  1. Chemistry
  2. Medicine
  3. Peace
  4. Physics

Answer: D) Physics

C. V. Raman won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930 for his work on the scattering of light and the discovery of the Raman Effect. He was the first Asian to win a Nobel in science.

10. The Jnanpith Award is given for outstanding contribution in which field?

  1. Literature
  2. Science
  3. Cinema
  4. Sports

Answer: A) Literature

The Jnanpith Award is India's highest literary award, given for outstanding contribution to literature in any of the languages of the Eighth Schedule. It was instituted in 1961 and first awarded in 1965.

  1. Art

  3. Sports
  4. Science

15. The Nobel Prizes were first awarded in which year?

  1. 1895
  2. 1910
  3. 1901
  4. 1921

Answer: C) 1901

The Nobel Prizes were first awarded in 1901, five years after Alfred Nobel's death in 1896, as per his will. The Economics prize was added later, in 1969, by the Swedish central bank.

  2. Param Vir Chakra
  3. Kirti Chakra
  4. Shaurya Chakra

Answer: A) Ashoka Chakra
