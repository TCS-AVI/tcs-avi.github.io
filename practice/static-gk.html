1. The motto 'Satyameva Jayate' on the national emblem is taken from which text?

  1. Rigveda
  2. Bhagavad Gita
  3. Chandogya Upanishad
  4. Mundaka Upanishad

Answer: D) Mundaka Upanishad

Satyameva Jayate (Truth alone triumphs) is from the Mundaka Upanishad and is written in Devanagari under the Lion Capital. It was adopted with the emblem on 26 January 1950.
