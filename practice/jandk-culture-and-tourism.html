1. Match List I with List II. List I (Festival or event) A. Hemis Tsechu B. Jhiri Mela C. Khir Bhawani Mela D. Urs at Charar-e-Sharief List II (Association) 1. Baba Jitto 2. Sheikh Noor-ud-Din Noorani 3. Padmasambhava 4. Goddess Ragnya Devi Choose the correct answer.

  1. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2
  2. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  3. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2
  4. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2

Answer: D) A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2

Hemis Tsechu in Ladakh honours Guru Padmasambhava. Jhiri Mela near Jammu commemorates the sacrifice of Baba Jitto. Khir Bhawani Mela at Tulmulla honours Goddess Ragnya Devi. The Urs at Charar-e-Sharief marks Nund Rishi, Sheikh Noor-ud-Din Noorani. So A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2.

2. Consider the following statements about Kashmiri handicrafts: 1. Pashmina wool is obtained from the Changthangi goat. 2. Kani shawls are woven with wooden bobbins and are associated with Kanihama in Budgam. 3. Sozni is a form of papier-mache work. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 3 only
  2. 2 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1 and 2 only

Answer: D) 1 and 2 only

Pashmina fibre comes from the Changthangi goat of Ladakh, so statement 1 is true. Kani shawls are woven on looms using small wooden spools called kanis and are linked with Kanihama, so statement 2 is true. Sozni is fine needle embroidery on shawls, not papier-mache, so statement 3 is false.

3. Assertion (A): Wazwan is traditionally served on a large copper plate called Traem to a group of four persons. Reason (R): Gushtaba is traditionally the final dish of a Wazwan. Select the correct answer.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A

Assertion is true: the Traem is the shared copper platter for four diners. Reason is also true: Gushtaba, minced meat balls in curd gravy, closes the feast. However, the last course has nothing to do with the serving plate, so R does not explain A.

4. Match List I with List II. List I (Mughal garden) A. Shalimar Bagh B. Nishat Bagh C. Chashme Shahi D. Pari Mahal List II (Associated builder) 1. Asaf Khan 2. Ali Mardan Khan 3. Dara Shikoh 4. Jahangir Choose the correct answer.

  1. A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
  2. A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2
  3. A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3
  4. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4

Answer: C) A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3

Shalimar Bagh was laid out by Jahangir for Nur Jahan. Nishat Bagh was built by Asaf Khan, her brother. Chashme Shahi was built by the governor Ali Mardan Khan under Shah Jahan. Pari Mahal is linked to the Mughal prince Dara Shikoh. So A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3.

5. Match List I with List II. List I (Craft) A. Kani B. Pinjrakari C. Namda D. Gabba List II (Description) 1. Felted woollen floor covering 2. Lattice work in wood 3. Shawl woven with wooden bobbins 4. Embroidered rug made of recycled woollen fabric Choose the correct answer.

  1. A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1
  2. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  3. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
  4. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4

Answer: B) A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4

Kani is a shawl woven with wooden spools. Pinjrakari is the nail-free lattice work used in windows and screens. Namda is a felted rug made from wool pressed without weaving. Gabba is an embroidered rug made from recycled woollen cloth. So A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4.
