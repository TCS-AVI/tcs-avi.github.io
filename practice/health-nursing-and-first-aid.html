1. Which position is used for a patient in cardiac or respiratory distress to help breathing?

  1. Trendelenburg position
  2. Prone position
  3. Lithotomy position
  4. Fowler's position

Answer: D) Fowler's position

The head of the bed is raised 45 to 60 degrees.
