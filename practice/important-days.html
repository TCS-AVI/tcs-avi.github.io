1. Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (31 October) marks the birth anniversary of which leader?

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru
  2. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
  3. Subhas Chandra Bose
  4. Lal Bahadur Shastri

Answer: B) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

National Unity Day is observed on 31 October to honour Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, who led the integration of the princely states.

3. August Kranti Day (Quit India Day) is observed on which date?

  1. 9 August
  2. 15 August
  3. 8 August
  4. 26 January

Answer: A) 9 August

August Kranti Day is observed on 9 August, as the Quit India Movement was launched on 9 August 1942 from Gowalia Tank, Mumbai. The resolution was passed on 8 August, and the call was Do or Die.
