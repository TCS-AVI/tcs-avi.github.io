1. Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (31 October) marks the birth anniversary of which leader?

  2. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
  3. Subhas Chandra Bose
  4. Lal Bahadur Shastri

Answer: B) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

National Unity Day is observed on 31 October to honour Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, who led the integration of the princely states.
