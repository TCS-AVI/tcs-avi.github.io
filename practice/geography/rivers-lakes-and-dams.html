1. Match the following lakes with their features: A. Wular B. Sambhar C. Pulicat D. Loktak 1. Inland salt lake of Rajasthan 2. Floating biomass called phumdis, Manipur 3. Freshwater lake of tectonic origin in Jammu and Kashmir 4. Lagoon on the Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu coast

  1. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2
  2. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  3. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2
  4. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2

Answer: D) A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2

Wular in Kashmir is a large freshwater lake of tectonic origin. Sambhar is India's largest inland salt lake in Rajasthan. Pulicat is a coastal lagoon shared by Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu, separated from the sea by Sriharikota island. Loktak in Manipur is known for its floating phumdis. So A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2.

2. Consider the following statements: 1. Tea thrives on the black soils of the Deccan plateau. 2. Rubber requires a hot and humid climate with heavy rainfall, generally above 200 cm. 3. Jute grows best in the humid climate and alluvial soils of the Ganga-Brahmaputra delta. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 2 and 3 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: A) 2 and 3 only

Statement 1 is wrong: tea grows on well-drained, acidic hill slopes and highland soils, not black soil. Statement 2 is correct: rubber needs temperatures above about 25 degrees and heavy rain. Statement 3 is correct: jute needs warm, humid weather and fertile alluvium found in the delta region. So 2 and 3 only.

3. Consider the following statements: 1. Black soil is formed by weathering of basaltic rocks and is rich in lime, iron, magnesium and alumina. 2. Laterite soil is rich in nitrogen and humus because of heavy leaching. 3. Khadar is the newer alluvium of river floodplains and is more fertile than Bhangar. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: C) 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is correct: regur soil derives from Deccan basalt and has these minerals. Statement 2 is wrong: intense leaching in laterite removes nutrients, leaving it poor in nitrogen, humus and lime. Statement 3 is correct: Khadar is renewed by annual floods and is finer and more fertile than the older, kankar-bearing Bhangar. Hence 1 and 3 only.

4. Match the following rivers with their places of origin: A. Krishna B. Mahanadi C. Narmada D. Tapi 1. Amarkantak 2. Multai 3. Mahabaleshwar 4. Sihawa in Chhattisgarh

  1. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2
  2. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  3. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1
  4. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

Answer: D) A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

The Krishna rises near Mahabaleshwar in the Western Ghats. The Mahanadi rises near Sihawa in the Dhamtari district of Chhattisgarh. The Narmada rises at Amarkantak plateau. The Tapi rises near Multai in the Betul district of Madhya Pradesh. Hence A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2.

5. Assertion (A): The Thar desert receives scanty rainfall from the southwest monsoon. Reason (R): The Arabian Sea branch of the monsoon blows parallel to the Aravalli range and meets no barrier to force the moist air up. Choose the correct option:

  1. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: B) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

The Assertion is true: western Rajasthan lies in a rain shadow and gets very little monsoon rain. The Reason is also true: the Arabian Sea branch runs along the line of the Aravallis, so little orographic lifting occurs. Because the winds are not forced to rise and cool, they yield little rain, so R explains A.

6. Consider the following statements: 1. Kandla port in Gujarat was developed after Partition as a replacement for Karachi. 2. Mormugao port in Goa is situated at the mouth of the Zuari estuary. 3. Kolkata port is a natural harbour on the Arabian Sea coast. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1 and 3 only

Answer: B) 1 and 2 only

Statement 1 is correct: Kandla, a tidal port in the Gulf of Kachchh, was built after Partition. Statement 2 is correct: Mormugao lies at the entrance of the Zuari. Statement 3 is wrong: Kolkata is a riverine port on the Hooghly in the Bay of Bengal region, not on the Arabian Sea. So 1 and 2 only.

7. Consider the following statements: 1. The river Tapi rises near Amarkantak in Madhya Pradesh. 2. The Narmada flows westward through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges. 3. The Luni river of Rajasthan flows into the Arabian Sea. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 2 and 3 only

Answer: B) 2 only

Statement 1 is wrong: the Tapi rises near Multai in Betul district, while Amarkantak is the source of the Narmada and Son. Statement 2 is correct: the Narmada occupies a rift valley and drains west into the Gulf of Khambhat. Statement 3 is wrong: the Luni rises in the Aravallis near Ajmer and ends in the marshes of the Rann of Kachchh without reaching the sea.

8. Consider the following statements: 1. The Benguela Current is a warm current off the south-western coast of Africa. 2. The Oyashio Current is a warm current of the north Pacific. 3. The Agulhas Current is a warm current flowing along the south-eastern coast of Africa. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 3 only
  4. 2 and 3 only

Answer: C) 3 only

Statement 1 is wrong: the Benguela Current is cold, bringing cool water up the south-west African coast. Statement 2 is wrong: the Oyashio (Kuril) Current is cold and flows south from the Bering Sea. Statement 3 is correct: the Agulhas Current carries warm water from the Indian Ocean along Africa's south-east coast. Only statement 3 is true.

12. The Humboldt (Peru) Current is a:

  1. Warm current
  2. Cold current
  3. Tidal current
  4. Monsoon drift

Answer: B) Cold current

The Humboldt or Peru Current is a cold current along the west coast of South America. It brings nutrient-rich water, supports fisheries, and helps create the Atacama desert. Its warming is linked to El Nino.
