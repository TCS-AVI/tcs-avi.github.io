1. Consider the following statements: 1. National Highway 44 connects Srinagar with Kanniyakumari and is the longest national highway in India. 2. The Golden Quadrilateral links Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata. 3. The East-West Corridor connects Silchar in Assam with Porbandar in Gujarat. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: D) 1, 2 and 3

Statement 1 is correct: NH 44 runs from Srinagar to Kanniyakumari, the longest highway. Statement 2 is correct: the Golden Quadrilateral joins the four metros. Statement 3 is correct: the East-West Corridor of NHDP runs from Silchar to Porbandar. All three statements are true.

2. Consider the following statements: 1. The National Disaster Management Authority is chaired by the Prime Minister. 2. The National Executive Committee under the Disaster Management Act 2005 is chaired by the Union Home Minister. 3. The National Disaster Response Force was constituted under the Disaster Management Act 2005. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1 only

Answer: B) 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is correct: the Act makes the Prime Minister the chairperson of NDMA. Statement 2 is wrong: the National Executive Committee is headed by the Union Home Secretary. Statement 3 is correct: Section 44 of the Act provided for the NDRF. So 1 and 3 only.

3. Consider the following statements: 1. Uttar Pradesh is the most populous state of India as per Census 2011. 2. Goa is the least populous state of India as per Census 2011. 3. Bihar has the highest population density among the states as per Census 2011. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is correct: Uttar Pradesh has about 20 crore people. Statement 2 is wrong: Sikkim, with about 6 lakh people, is the least populous state; Goa is slightly larger. Statement 3 is correct: Bihar has a density of about 1100 persons per sq km, the highest among states. So 1 and 3 only.

4. Consider the following statements: 1. As per Census 2011, the sex ratio of India was 943 females per 1000 males. 2. As per Census 2011, the literacy rate of India was 74.04 per cent. 3.   1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: Census 2011 recorded an overall sex ratio of 943 and a literacy rate of 74.04 per cent. Statement 3 is wrong: the child sex ratio fell to 919, lower than the overall sex ratio. So only 1 and 2 are true.

5. Assertion (A): Black soil retains moisture for a long time. Reason (R): Black soil contains a very high proportion of sand and very little clay. Choose the correct option:

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: C) A is true but R is false

The Assertion is true: regur soil swells when wet and cracks when dry and holds moisture well, which suits cotton. The Reason is false: black soil is clayey, with fine particles, not sandy. It is the high clay content that gives its high water-holding capacity.
