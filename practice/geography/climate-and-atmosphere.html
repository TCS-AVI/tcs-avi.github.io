1. Consider the following statements: 1. Mediterranean climate is found on the western margins of continents roughly between 30 and 40 degrees latitude. 2. The Veld is the temperate grassland of Australia. 3. The Downs are the temperate grasslands of Australia. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 3 only
  4. 1 and 3 only

Answer: D) 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is correct: hot dry summers and mild wet winters occur on west coasts at 30-40 degrees. Statement 2 is wrong: the Veld is the South African grassland, while Australia's grassland is the Downs. Statement 3 is correct, as in the Darling Downs. So 1 and 3 only.

2. Consider the following statements: 1. The Tropical Easterly Jet is associated with the onset and burst of the southwest monsoon over peninsular India. 2. Western disturbances that bring winter rain to north-western India originate over the Bay of Bengal. 3. Mawsynram receives most of its rainfall from the retreating monsoon. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: A) 1 only

Statement 1 is correct: the upper-air easterly jet over the peninsula strengthens the monsoon onset. Statement 2 is wrong because western disturbances originate over the Mediterranean region. Statement 3 is wrong because Mawsynram gets its heavy rain from the southwest (advancing) monsoon branch of the Bay of Bengal. Only statement 1 is true.

3. Consider the following statements: 1. The Tropic of Cancer passes through Odisha. 2. The Standard Meridian of India, 82 degrees 30 minutes East, passes through Mirzapur in Uttar Pradesh. 3. Indira Point, the southernmost point of India, lies in Great Nicobar. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 3 only

Answer: B) 2 and 3 only

Statement 1 is wrong: the Tropic of Cancer crosses Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram, not Odisha. Statement 2 is correct: the IST meridian runs near Mirzapur. Statement 3 is correct: Indira Point is in the Great Nicobar island. So 2 and 3 only.

5. Which line is approximately along 180° longitude?

  1. Prime Meridian
  2. Equator
  3. Tropic of Capricorn
  4. International Date Line

Answer: D) International Date Line

The International Date Line runs roughly along 180 degrees longitude through the Pacific, bending to avoid land. Crossing it from west to east you subtract a day; east to west you add a day.

6. The southwest monsoon normally reaches Kerala around:

  1. 1 June
  2. 1 May
  3. 15 July
  4. 1 September

Answer: A) 1 June

The southwest monsoon normally hits the Kerala coast around 1 June, the standard onset date used by IMD. It then advances north and covers the whole country by about 8 July. Remember: June 1 = Kerala, July 8 = all India.

7. Which crop is sown in the kharif season?

  1. Wheat
  2. Barley
  3. Rice
  4. Mustard

Answer: C) Rice

Kharif crops are sown with the onset of monsoon in June-July and harvested in September-October. Rice, maize, cotton, jowar, bajra and groundnut are kharif crops. Wheat, barley and mustard are rabi crops sown in winter. Tip: rabi = winter.

9. Hot dry winds blowing over the northern plains in summer are called:

  1. Mango showers
  2. Kalbaisakhi
  3. Bardoichila
  4. Loo

Answer: D) Loo

Loo is a strong, hot, dry wind that blows over the northwestern plains, especially Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan and UP, during May-June afternoons. It can cause heat stroke. Do not confuse with Mango showers (south) and Kalbaisakhi (east).

10. Winter rainfall in northwestern India, helpful for wheat, is mainly caused by:

  1. Retreating monsoon
  2. Western Disturbances
  3. Loo
  4. Nor'westers

Answer: B) Western Disturbances

Western Disturbances are extratropical cyclonic systems originating over the Mediterranean region. They bring winter rain and snow to Punjab, Haryana, J&K and nearby areas, which is very good for rabi crops like wheat. Tip: W for Western, W for Winter, W for Wheat.

11. The Prime Meridian passes through:

  1. Paris
  2. Cairo
  3. Greenwich, London
  4. Washington D.C.

Answer: C) Greenwich, London

The Prime Meridian, 0 degree longitude, passes through the Royal Observatory at Greenwich, London. Longitude is measured east and west of it, and it is the basis for GMT.

13. Kalbaisakhi (Nor'westers) are thunderstorms of which region?

  1. Rajasthan
  2. West Bengal
  3. Punjab
  4. Kerala

Answer: B) West Bengal

Kalbaisakhi, meaning calamity of the month of Baisakh, is a violent pre-monsoon thunderstorm with squalls and hail. It is most associated with West Bengal, with similar storms called Bardoichila in Assam. They are useful for tea, jute and rice.
