1. Consider the following statements about the Instrument of Accession of 1947: I. It gave the Dominion of India authority over defence, external affairs and communications. II. It was signed by Maharaja Hari Singh on 15 August 1947. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. I only
  2. II only
  3. Both I and II
  4. Neither I nor II

Answer: A) I only

Statement I is correct: the Instrument covered defence, external affairs and communications along with ancillary matters. Statement II is wrong: Hari Singh signed it on 26 October 1947, and Governor-General Mountbatten accepted it on 27 October. Hence only I is correct.

2. Consider the following statements about the events of 2019: 1. The Constitution (Application to Jammu and Kashmir) Order, 2019 was issued by the President on 5 August 2019. 2. The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act received Presidential assent on 9 August 2019. 3. The Union Territories of Jammu and Kashmir and of Ladakh came into existence on 31 October 2019. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: D) All of the above

The Presidential Order C.O. 272 was issued on 5 August 2019, so statement 1 is true. The Reorganisation Act got assent on 9 August 2019, so statement 2 is true. The appointed day for the two Union Territories was 31 October 2019, so statement 3 is true. All three are correct.

3. Consider the following statements about the Panchayati Raj amendments: 1. The 73rd Amendment inserted Part IX and the Eleventh Schedule with 29 subjects. 2. The 74th Amendment inserted Part IXA and the Twelfth Schedule with 18 subjects. 3. Article 243K provides for the State Finance Commission. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 2 only
  2. 1 only
  3. 1 and 2 only
  4. 2 and 3 only

Answer: C) 1 and 2 only

The 73rd Amendment added Part IX and the Eleventh Schedule listing 29 subjects, so statement 1 is true. The 74th added Part IXA and the Twelfth Schedule with 18 subjects, so statement 2 is true. The State Finance Commission is under Article 243I; Article 243K deals with the State Election Commission, so statement 3 is false.

  1. Kashmiri
  2. Dogri
  3. Gojri
  4. Pahari

The 92nd Amendment of 2003 added Bodo, Dogri, Maithili and Santhali to the Eighth Schedule. Dogri is the main language of the Jammu region. Kashmiri had been listed from the beginning, while Gojri and Pahari are not in the Schedule.

7. Consider the following statements about Article 370: 1. It was placed in Part XXI of the Constitution dealing with temporary, transitional and special provisions. 2.   1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
