1. Assertion (A): The clear sky appears blue in colour. Reason (R): Blue light has a longer wavelength than red light and is therefore scattered more by air molecules. Choose the correct option.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: C) A is true but R is false

A is true: air molecules scatter sunlight and blue dominates what reaches our eyes. R is false: blue light has a shorter wavelength than red. Rayleigh scattering is inversely proportional to the fourth power of wavelength, so shorter blue wavelengths scatter more. R therefore wrongly states the wavelength relationship.

2. Which one of the following statements is NOT correct?

  1. The mass of a body remains the same on the Earth and on the Moon
  2. The weight of a body on the Moon is about one-sixth of its weight on the Earth
  3. The acceleration due to gravity has the same value at the equator and at the poles
  4. The acceleration due to gravity decreases with increasing height above the Earth's surface

Answer: C) The acceleration due to gravity has the same value at the equator and at the poles

Mass is intrinsic and does not change with location. Moon's gravity is about one-sixth of Earth's, so weight is about one-sixth. Earth is oblate and rotating, so g is slightly greater at the poles than at the equator; the statement of equality is incorrect. g also falls with altitude.

3. Consider the following statements: 1. Sound waves in air are longitudinal waves. 2. The speed of sound in air is independent of temperature. 3. Sound travels faster in water than in air. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: B) 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is true: air particles vibrate along the direction of travel. Statement 2 is false: it increases as temperature rises. Statement 3 is true: about 1480 m/s against about 340 m/s. So the answer is 1 and 3 only.

4. Consider the following statements: 1. Total internal reflection can occur when light travels from a denser to a rarer medium at an angle of incidence greater than the critical angle. 2. Optical fibres transmit signals by total internal reflection. 3. The sparkle of a diamond is mainly due to its low refractive index. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 2 and 3 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 1, 2 and 3
  4. 1 and 2 only

Answer: D) 1 and 2 only

Statement 1 is true: this is the exact condition. Statement 2 is true: light stays trapped in the core. Statement 3 is false: its refractive index is high, giving a small critical angle. So the answer is 1 and 2 only.

5. Approximately what is the speed of light in vacuum?

  1. 3 × 10⁶ m/s
  2. 3 × 10⁵ m/s
  3. 3 × 10⁸ m/s
  4. 3 × 10¹⁰ m/s

Answer: C) 3 × 10⁸ m/s

Light travels in vacuum at about 3 x 10^8 m/s (299,792,458 m/s exactly), or about 3 lakh km/s. Light from the Sun takes roughly 8 minutes to reach Earth.

6. What is the boiling point of water at standard pressure on the Kelvin scale?

  1. 273 K
  2. 373 K
  3. 100 K
  4. 473 K

Answer: B) 373 K

Kelvin = Celsius + 273, so water boils at 100 + 273 = 373 K at standard pressure. Water freezes at 273 K. Tip: SI temperature unit kelvin has no degree sign.

12. At what temperature do the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales show the same reading?

  1. −40 degrees
  2. 0 degrees
  3. 32 degrees
  4. −32 degrees

Answer: A) −40 degrees

Setting C = F in F = (9/5)C + 32 gives C = -40, so -40 degrees is the one point where both scales agree. Tip: freezing is 0 C or 32 F; boiling 100 C or 212 F.

13. What is the SI unit of magnetic flux density?

  1. Weber
  2. Henry
  3. Tesla
  4. Farad

Answer: C) Tesla

Tesla (T) is the SI unit of magnetic flux density (magnetic field B), equal to one weber per square metre. Weber measures magnetic flux itself, and henry measures inductance.

14. The Raman Effect, for which an Indian scientist won the Nobel Prize in Physics, concerns what?

  1. Reflection of sound
  2. Scattering of light
  3. Radioactive decay
  4. Electromagnetic induction

20. According to Ohm's law, at constant temperature the current through a conductor is directly proportional to what?

  1. Its resistance
  2. Potential difference across it
  3. Its length
  4. Its cross-sectional area

Answer: B) Potential difference across it

Ohm's law states V = IR, so at constant temperature the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across it. Tip: more voltage, more current.

  1. Diamond
  2. Graphite
  3. Fullerene C-60 crystal
  4. Charcoal powder is an insulator

Answer: B) Graphite

Graphite conducts electricity because each carbon is bonded to three others, leaving one free electron per atom between layers. Diamond has no free electrons and is an insulator.
