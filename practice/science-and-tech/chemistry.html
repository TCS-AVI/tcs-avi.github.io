1. Match List I with List II. List I: A. Washing soda; B. Baking soda; C. Bleaching powder; D. Plaster of Paris List II: 1. CaOCl2; 2. Na2CO3.10H2O; 3. CaSO4.1/2H2O; 4. NaHCO3 Choose the correct combination.

  1. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  2. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
  3. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
  4. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1

Answer: B) A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3

Washing soda is sodium carbonate decahydrate, Na2CO3.10H2O (A-2). Baking soda is sodium bicarbonate, NaHCO3 (B-4). Bleaching powder is calcium oxychloride, CaOCl2 (C-1). Plaster of Paris is calcium sulphate hemihydrate, CaSO4.1/2H2O (D-3). Hence A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3.

2. Consider the following statements: 1. Brass is an alloy of copper and zinc. 2. Bronze is an alloy of copper and zinc. 3. Stainless steel contains chromium. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: C) 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is true: correct composition. Statement 2 is false: bronze is copper and tin. Statement 3 is true: chromium gives corrosion resistance. So the answer is 1 and 3 only.

3. Consider the following statements: 1. Pure water has a pH of 7 at 25 degrees Celsius. 2. Rain is generally termed acid rain when its pH falls below about 5.6. 3. Plaster of Paris is calcium sulphate dihydrate (CaSO4.2H2O). Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: A) 1 and 2 only

Statement 1 is true: it is neutral. Statement 2 is true: normal rain is about 5.6 due to dissolved CO2. Statement 3 is false: it is the hemihydrate, CaSO4.1/2H2O; gypsum is the dihydrate. So the answer is 1 and 2 only.

  3. Calcium carbonate
  4. Calcium sulphate

Answer: A) Calcium hydroxide

  1. Ovary
  2. Style base
  3. Stigma

Answer: C) Stigma

Pollination is the transfer of pollen from the anther to the stigma. The stigma is the sticky tip of the carpel, after which the pollen tube grows through the style to the ovary. It can be self or cross pollination.

9. What is the atomic number of carbon?

  1. 12
  2. 6
  3. 8
  4. 4

Answer: B) 6

Atomic number equals the number of protons; carbon has 6. Its common mass number is 12 (6 protons plus 6 neutrons). Oxygen has atomic number 8.

12. Who is credited with creating the first widely accepted periodic table?

  1. John Dalton
  2. Antoine Lavoisier
  3. Marie Curie
  4. Dmitri Mendeleev

Answer: D) Dmitri Mendeleev

19. Plaster of Paris is chemically which compound?

  1. Calcium sulphate dihydrate
  2. Calcium sulphate hemihydrate
  3. Calcium carbonate
  4. Magnesium sulphate

Answer: B) Calcium sulphate hemihydrate

Plaster of Paris is calcium sulphate hemihydrate, CaSO4.1/2H2O, made by heating gypsum (CaSO4.2H2O) at about 373 K. It sets into hard gypsum on adding water and is used for casts.

23. Which gas is the main cause of acid rain?

  1. Sulphur dioxide
  2. Oxygen
  3. Argon

31. The principle of floatation is based on which law?

  1. Pascal's law
  2. Bernoulli's principle
  3. Archimedes' principle
  4. Boyle's law

Answer: C) Archimedes' principle

Archimedes' principle says a body immersed in fluid experiences an upward buoyant force equal to the weight of fluid displaced. A body floats when buoyant force equals its weight.
