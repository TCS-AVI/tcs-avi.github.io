1. Consider the following statements: 1. BrahMos is a supersonic cruise missile developed jointly by India and Russia. 2. Prithvi is a surface-to-air missile. 3. Nag is an anti-tank guided missile developed by DRDO. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 3 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: A) 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is true: a DRDO-NPOM venture. Statement 2 is false: it is a surface-to-surface ballistic missile. Statement 3 is true: a fire-and-forget type. So the answer is 1 and 3 only.

4. What is the approximate escape velocity from Earth's surface?

  1. 7.9 km/s
  2. 11.2 km/s
  3. 9.8 km/s
  4. 3.0 km/s

Answer: B) 11.2 km/s

Escape velocity from Earth's surface is about 11.2 km/s, independent of the mass of the object. Orbital velocity near the surface is about 7.9 km/s. The Moon's escape velocity is about 2.4 km/s.
