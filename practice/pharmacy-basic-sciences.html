1. Dietary deficiency of iodine leads to

  1. simple (endemic) goitre
  2. acromegaly
  3. Cushing syndrome
  4. Graves disease

Answer: A) simple (endemic) goitre

Lack of iodine lowers thyroid hormones; raised TSH enlarges the gland.

  1. Riboflavin
  2. Niacin
  3. Cobalamin
  4. Thiamine

Answer: D) Thiamine

Wernicke's encephalopathy results from thiamine deficiency.

  1. Lagging strand
  2. Leading strand

Answer: A) Lagging strand

The lagging strand is made discontinuously as short fragments that DNA ligase joins.

  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A

Both are true, but pressurised steam is a separate fact and does not explain spore resistance.

9. Scurvy results from deficiency of which vitamin?

  1. Riboflavin
  2. Retinol
  3. Ascorbic acid (vitamin C)

Vitamin C is needed for hydroxylation of proline and lysine in collagen, so deficiency causes bleeding gums and poor wound healing.
