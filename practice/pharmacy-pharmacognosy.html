1. The antidiabetic drug metformin was developed from guanidine derivatives found in:

  1. Gymnema sylvestre
  2. Galega officinalis
  3. Momordica charantia
  4. Trigonella foenum-graecum

Answer: B) Galega officinalis

Metformin originated from the guanidine-containing plant Galega officinalis.
