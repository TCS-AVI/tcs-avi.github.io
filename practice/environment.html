1. Consider the following statements: 1. CITES Appendix I lists species threatened with extinction, and commercial trade in them is generally prohibited. 2. Appendix II prohibits all international trade in the listed species. 3. CITES came into force in 1975. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is true: trade only in exceptional cases. Statement 2 is false: it permits regulated trade through export permits. Statement 3 is true: it was drawn up in 1973 in Washington. So the answer is 1 and 3 only.

16. Assertion (A): Excess nutrient enrichment of a lake can lead to the death of fish. Reason (R): Decomposition of dead algal bloom by aerobic bacteria depletes dissolved oxygen in the water. Choose the correct option.

  1. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  2. A is true but R is false
  3. A is false but R is true
  4. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Answer: D) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Nutrient enrichment (eutrophication) causes algal blooms. When the algae die, aerobic bacteria decompose them and consume dissolved oxygen. The resulting hypoxia suffocates fish. A is true, R is true, and R directly explains why fish die.

  1. Primary consumers
  2. Secondary consumers

36. According to Lindeman's 10 percent law, how much energy is passed to the next trophic level?

  1. About 50%
  2. About 90%
  3. About 1%
  4. About 10%

Answer: D) About 10%

Lindeman's ten percent law says only about 10 percent of energy passes from one trophic level to the next; the rest is lost as heat in respiration and other processes. It limits food chains to few levels.

49. A biosphere reserve has which zones?

  2. Urban, rural and wild
  3. Core, buffer and transition
  4. Primary and secondary only

  2. Near Threatened
  3. Least Concern
