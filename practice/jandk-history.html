1. Consider the following statements about the Dogra rulers of Jammu and Kashmir: 1. Ranbir Singh succeeded Gulab Singh in 1857. 2. Pratap Singh ruled the State after Ranbir Singh. 3. Hari Singh was the son of Maharaja Pratap Singh. Which of the statements given above is/are not correct?

  1. 3 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 only
  4. 1 and 2 only

Answer: A) 3 only

Gulab Singh died in 1857 and was succeeded by his son Ranbir Singh, so statement 1 is true. Pratap Singh, Ranbir Singh's son, took over in 1885, so statement 2 is true. Hari Singh was Pratap Singh's nephew, being the son of Amar Singh, so statement 3 is incorrect.

2. Consider the following statements about the Treaty of Amritsar: 1. It was signed on 16 March 1846 between the British East India Company and Gulab Singh. 2. Gulab Singh paid 75 lakh Nanakshahi rupees under the treaty. 3. The territory transferred lay to the west of the Indus and east of the Ravi. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 2 and 3 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: B) 1 and 2 only

Statement 1 is correct: the treaty was concluded on 16 March 1846. Statement 2 is correct: the price was 75 lakh Nanakshahi rupees. Statement 3 is wrong: the ceded hill country lay east of the Indus and west of the Ravi. Hence only 1 and 2 are correct.

3. Consider the following statements about the rivers of Jammu and Kashmir: 1. The Jhelum rises from the Verinag spring in Anantnag district. 2. Wular Lake is fed mainly by the Chenab. 3. The ancient name of the Chenab was Asikni. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 3 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: A) 1 and 3 only

The Jhelum (Vitasta) emerges at Verinag, so statement 1 is true. Wular Lake is fed by the Jhelum, which flows through it, not by the Chenab, so statement 2 is false. The Chenab was Asikni in Vedic texts and Acesines to the Greeks, so statement 3 is true.

4. Under the Treaty of Amritsar, Gulab Singh was to present an annual token of British supremacy. Consider the following statements: 1. Two horses of approved quality 2. Twelve shawl goats of approved breed, six male and six female 3. Three pairs of Kashmir shawls Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only

Answer: C) 2 and 3 only

The token tribute was one horse, twelve shawl goats (six male, six female) and three pairs of Kashmir shawls. Statement 1 is wrong because the treaty specified one horse, not two. Statements 2 and 3 are correct.

5. All of the following pairs of monument and builder are correctly matched EXCEPT

  1. Martand Sun Temple - Zain-ul-Abidin
  2. Jamia Masjid, Srinagar - Sultan Sikandar
  3. Avantipur temples - Avantivarman
  4. Rajatarangini - Kalhana

Answer: A) Martand Sun Temple - Zain-ul-Abidin

The Martand Sun Temple in Anantnag is attributed to Lalitaditya Muktapida of the Karkota dynasty, not to Zain-ul-Abidin. The Jamia Masjid was built by Sultan Sikandar, the Avantipur temples by Avantivarman of the Utpala dynasty, and the Rajatarangini was composed by Kalhana.

6. The Glancy Commission, appointed after the 1931 disturbances in Kashmir, was followed by the creation of which body in 1934?

  1. Constituent Assembly
  2. Legislative Council
  3. Panchayati Raj Institutions under the 1989 Act
  4. Praja Sabha

Answer: D) Praja Sabha

After the 1931 agitation, the Glancy Commission examined grievances and recommended reforms. This led to the Constitution Act of 1934 and the setting up of the Praja Sabha, the first legislative assembly of the State, partly elected.
