1. Assertion (A): Article 19 freedoms are automatically suspended whenever a National Emergency is declared, on any ground. Reason (R): The 44th Amendment replaced 'internal disturbance' with 'armed rebellion' as a ground for Emergency. Select the correct answer:

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: D) A is false but R is true

A is false: under Article 358 as amended by the 44th Amendment, Article 19 is suspended only when Emergency is declared on grounds of war or external aggression, not armed rebellion. R is true, as the 44th Amendment made this substitution in Article 352.

4. Assertion (A): The Rajya Sabha is a permanent House not subject to dissolution. Reason (R): One-third of its members retire every second year. Select the correct answer:

  1. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: B) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

A is true because Article 83(1) says the Rajya Sabha is not subject to dissolution. R is also true, since one-third retire every second year under Article 83(1) read with the law. The staggered retirement keeps the House continuous, so R explains A.

12. Which one of the following statements about the Vice-President of India is NOT correct?

  1. He is ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
  2. He must be qualified for election as a member of the Rajya Sabha.
  3. Elected members of State Legislative Assemblies are part of the electoral college that elects him.
  4. He is elected by an electoral college consisting of members of both Houses of Parliament.

Answer: C) Elected members of State Legislative Assemblies are part of the electoral college that elects him.

Article 63 and 64 make the Vice-President ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha. Article 66(3) requires qualification for membership of the Rajya Sabha. Article 66(1) provides that the electoral college consists of members of both Houses of Parliament. State Assembly members do not vote, so that statement is incorrect.

15. Which one of the following is NOT a Directive Principle of State Policy?

  2. Uniform civil code for citizens (Article 44)
  3. Organisation of village panchayats (Article 40)
  4. Separation of judiciary from executive in public services (Article 50)

Answer: A) Equality before law and equal protection of laws (Article 14)

Articles 36 to 51 in Part IV contain the Directive Principles. Article 40 on village panchayats, Article 44 on a uniform civil code and Article 50 on separating the judiciary from the executive are all in Part IV. Article 14 is a Fundamental Right in Part III.
