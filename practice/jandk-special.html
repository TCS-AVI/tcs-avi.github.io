48. Operation Meghdoot, to take control of the Siachen Glacier, was launched in which year?

  1. 1984
  2. 1971
  3. 1987
  4. 1999

Answer: A) 1984

Operation Meghdoot was launched on 13 April 1984, when the Indian Army secured the Siachen heights, including the Saltoro Ridge. It was named after Kalidasa's Meghdoot. Do not confuse with Operation Vijay (Kargil, 1999).

49. Navreh is the New Year celebrated by which community?

  1. Gujjars
  2. Kashmiri Pandits
  3. Dogras
  4. Ladakhi Buddhists

Answer: B) Kashmiri Pandits

Navreh is the Kashmiri Pandit New Year, celebrated on the first day of Chaitra Navratri. A thali with rice, curd and a pen is seen as an auspicious ritual.
