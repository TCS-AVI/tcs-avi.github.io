1. The Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir came into existence under the J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019 on which date?

  1. 5 August 2019
  2. 9 August 2019
  3. 31 October 2019
  4. 26 January 2020

Answer: C) 31 October 2019

The Act received assent on 9 August 2019, and the UTs of J&K and Ladakh came into existence on 31 October 2019.
