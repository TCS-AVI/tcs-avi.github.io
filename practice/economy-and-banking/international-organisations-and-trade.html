1. IPO stands for:

  1. Initial Private Offer
  2. Indian Public Order
  3. Internal Profit Option
  4. Initial Public Offering

Answer: D) Initial Public Offering

An IPO is the first sale of a company's shares to the public.

2. Abhijit Banerjee shared the Nobel Prize in Economics in 2019 for work on:

  1. Monetary policy
  2. Game theory
  3. Alleviating global poverty
  4. International trade

Answer: C) Alleviating global poverty

Abhijit Banerjee shared the 2019 economics Nobel with Esther Duflo and Michael Kremer for an experimental approach to alleviating global poverty. Memory tip: 2019, poverty, randomised trials.

3. Shares are sold to the public for the first time in the:

  1. Secondary market
  2. Money market
  3. Primary market
  4. Futures market

Answer: C) Primary market

The primary market is where new securities are issued for the first time, as in an IPO. Later trading among investors takes place in the secondary market, such as stock exchanges.

9. India is described as a 'mixed economy' because:

  1. Only the private sector operates
  2. Only the government runs businesses
  3. It has no foreign trade
  4. Both the public and private sectors coexist

Answer: D) Both the public and private sectors coexist

India is a mixed economy because the public and private sectors coexist. The government runs key sectors, while private enterprise operates in others, within a framework of regulation.

11. The money market deals in short-term funds with maturity up to:

  1. Three years
  2. One year
  3. Five years
  4. Ten years

Answer: B) One year

The money market deals in short-term funds with maturity up to one year, with instruments like treasury bills, call money and commercial paper. Longer-term funds are raised in the capital market.

12. The excess of the value of imports of goods over exports of goods is called:

  1. Fiscal deficit
  2. Revenue deficit
  3. Primary deficit
  4. Trade deficit

Answer: D) Trade deficit

Trade deficit = imports minus exports (when imports are larger).
