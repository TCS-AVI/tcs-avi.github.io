1. Match the government scheme with its year of launch: A. Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana B. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana C. Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana D. Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY 1. 2018 2. 2014 3. 2016 4. 2015

  1. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  2. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
  3. A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1
  4. A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1

Answer: C) A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1

PMJDY for universal bank accounts was launched in August 2014. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana, the girl-child savings scheme, began in January 2015. PM Ujjwala Yojana for LPG connections to poor households started in May 2016. PM-JAY under Ayushman Bharat was launched in September 2018. So A-2, B-4, C-3, D-1.

2. Consider the following statements: 1. Atal Pension Yojana is aimed at workers in the unorganised sector. 2. PM-KISAN provides income support of Rs 6000 a year in three instalments to eligible farmer families. 3. Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin) is meant for housing the urban poor. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1 and 3 only

Answer: B) 1 and 2 only

Statement 1 is correct: APY provides a guaranteed pension to unorganised workers. Statement 2 is correct: PM-KISAN, launched in 2019, transfers Rs 6000 yearly in three equal instalments. Statement 3 is wrong: PMAY-Gramin targets rural households, while PMAY-Urban serves urban areas. So 1 and 2 only.

6. MGNREGA guarantees how many days of wage employment per rural household in a financial year?

  1. 100 days
  2. 50 days
  3. 150 days
  4. 200 days

Answer: A) 100 days

MGNREGA guarantees at least 100 days of wage employment per year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer for unskilled manual work. It was enacted in 2005 as NREGA and renamed in 2009.

17. The Competition Commission of India (CCI) was established under the Competition Act of:

  1. 1969
  2. 2002
  3. 1991
  4. 2013

Answer: B) 2002

The Competition Commission of India was established under the Competition Act, 2002, to prevent anti-competitive practices. The Act replaced the MRTP Act, 1969; the CCI became fully functional later.

18. The person who receives the insurance claim amount on the death of the insured is called the:

  1. Nominee/beneficiary
  2. Underwriter
  3. Surveyor
  4. Insurance agent

Answer: A) Nominee/beneficiary

The nominee or beneficiary is the person who receives the policy proceeds on the death of the insured. Underwriters assess risk, surveyors assess losses, and agents sell policies.
