1. Consider the following statements: 1. Article 112 of the Constitution requires the President to lay the Annual Financial Statement before Parliament. 2. The Public Account of India is established under Article 267. 3. A vote on account is passed to cover expenditure for the entire financial year. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 2 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1 only

Answer: D) 1 only

Statement 1 is correct: Article 112 provides for the annual financial statement, i.e. the Budget. Statement 2 is wrong: the Public Account is under Article 266(2), while Article 267 deals with the Contingency Fund. Statement 3 is wrong: a vote on account covers only a part of the year, usually a few months, pending the full budget. Only 1 is true.

2. Consider the following statements: 1. Stagflation is a situation of high inflation combined with stagnant output and high unemployment. 2. Cost-push inflation arises when the cost of production rises. 3. Disinflation means a fall in the general price level. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: A) 1 and 2 only

Statement 1 is correct: stagflation combines high inflation with slow growth and joblessness. Statement 2 is correct: rising wages or input prices push prices up from the supply side. Statement 3 is wrong: disinflation is a slowdown in the rate of inflation, while a fall in the general price level is deflation. So 1 and 2 only.

3. Consider the following statements: 1. Mining is classified as a part of the tertiary sector. 2. NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission with effect from 1 January 2015. 3. The Planning Commission was a constitutional body. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Statement 2 is correct: NITI Aayog came into being on 1 January 2015 as the policy think tank. 4. Which one of the following statements about national income concepts is NOT correct?

  1. GDP at market price equals GDP at factor cost plus indirect taxes minus subsidies
  2. GNP equals GDP plus net factor income from abroad
  3. Net Domestic Product equals GDP plus depreciation
  4. National income is NNP at factor cost

Answer: C) Net Domestic Product equals GDP plus depreciation

Market price GDP adds indirect taxes and subtracts subsidies to factor cost GDP, so the first is true. GNP adds net factor income from abroad to GDP, true. National income is conventionally NNP at factor cost, true. Net Domestic Product is GDP minus depreciation (capital consumption), so the third statement is incorrect.

5. Consider the following statements: 1. GST is a destination-based consumption tax. 2. GST was introduced through the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, which inserted Article 279A. 3. The GST Council is chaired by the Union Finance Minister. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1, 2 and 3
  3. 2 and 3 only

15. The situation of high inflation combined with high unemployment and slow growth is called:

  1. Deflation
  2. Reflation
  3. Disinflation
  4. Stagflation

Answer: D) Stagflation

Stagflation is the rare mix of stagnant growth, high unemployment and high inflation, seen in the 1970s oil shocks. It defied the Phillips curve idea that inflation and unemployment move opposite ways. Memory tip: stagnation + inflation = stagflation.

16. Per capita income is obtained by dividing:

  1. GDP by number of workers
  2. Total savings by population
  3. National income by total population
  4. Total exports by population

Answer: C) National income by total population

Per capita income is the average income per person. It is found by dividing national income by the total population of the country. It is a rough indicator of living standards, but it hides income inequality.

17. The Laffer curve illustrates the relationship between:

  1. Inflation and unemployment
  2. Income and consumption
  3. Tax rates and tax revenue
  4. Interest rate and savings

Answer: C) Tax rates and tax revenue

The Laffer curve shows that tax revenue rises with tax rate up to a point, after which very high rates discourage work and compliance and revenue falls. It is linked with supply-side economics and Arthur Laffer.

20. Gross National Product (GNP) equals GDP plus:

  1. Net factor income from abroad
  2. Indirect taxes
  3. Subsidies
  4. Depreciation

Answer: A) Net factor income from abroad

GNP counts income earned by a country's residents wherever they earn it. So GNP = GDP + net factor income from abroad (income earned abroad minus income paid to foreigners). Memory tip: N in GNP stands for National, so add the net factor income.

24. India's GST follows which model?

  1. Single GST levied only by Centre
  2. Single GST levied only by States
  3. Dual GST levied concurrently by Centre and States
  4. Provincial sales tax

Answer: C) Dual GST levied concurrently by Centre and States

India adopted a dual GST: the Centre and States both levy it concurrently on the same supply. Intra-state supplies attract CGST plus SGST, while inter-state supplies attract IGST collected by the Centre.

38. National income is also called:

  1. Gross Domestic Product at market price
  2. Net National Product at factor cost
  3. Gross National Product at market price
  4. Net Domestic Product at market price

Answer: B) Net National Product at factor cost

National income in Indian usage is Net National Product at factor cost, which equals the total factor incomes (wages, rent, interest, profit) earned by residents. It is obtained from GNP at market price by subtracting depreciation and net indirect taxes.

  1. Income of individuals
  2. Goods imported into or exported out of the country
  3. Agricultural land
  4. Sale of goods within a state

Answer: B) Goods imported into or exported out of the country

Customs duty is an indirect tax on goods brought into or taken out of India, governed by the Customs Act, 1962, and levied by the Union. Import duties are far more common than export duties.
