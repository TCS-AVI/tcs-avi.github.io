1. Consider the statements: 1. Class A items in ABC analysis are few in number but account for a large share of consumption value. 2. Vital items in VED analysis must always be available in stock. 3. Class C items require the strictest control as they form most of the total value. Which of the above are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: A) 1 and 2 only

Class A needs tightest control; class C items are many but low value.
