4. How many balls are bowled in a standard over in cricket?

  1. 6
  2. 4
  3. 5
  4. 8

Answer: A) 6

A standard over in cricket has six legal balls, after which another bowler bowls from the other end. Wides and no-balls are extra deliveries and do not count.
