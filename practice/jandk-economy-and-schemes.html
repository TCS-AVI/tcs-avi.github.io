1. Consider the following statements about Kashmir saffron: 1. It is grown from true seeds sown in spring. 2. It received a Geographical Indication tag in 2020. 3. It is harvested in autumn, around October and November. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: B) 2 and 3 only

Saffron (Crocus sativus) is sterile and propagated by corms, not seeds, so statement 1 is false. Kashmir saffron got its GI tag in 2020, so statement 2 is true. The flowers bloom and are picked in October-November, so statement 3 is true.
