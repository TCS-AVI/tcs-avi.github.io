1. Kalamkari textile art is associated with which state?

  1. Andhra Pradesh
  2. Gujarat
  3. Rajasthan
  4. Karnataka

Answer: A) Andhra Pradesh

Kalamkari is a hand-painted or block-printed textile art of Andhra Pradesh, with centres at Srikalahasti and Machilipatnam, using natural dyes. Its name means pen-work.

2. Sattriya, recognised as a classical dance in 2000, belongs to which state?

  1. Assam
  2. Manipur
  3. Nagaland
  4. Tripura

Answer: A) Assam

Sattriya was given classical status by the Sangeet Natak Akademi in 2000. It was developed by Srimanta Sankardeva in the 15th-16th century in Assam's Vaishnav monasteries called sattras.

  2. 92nd
  3. 56th
  4. 71st

The 71st Constitutional Amendment (1992) added Konkani, Manipuri and Nepali to the Eighth Schedule, raising the number to 18. Memory tip: 71st = 3 languages (K, M, N).

5. Lohri is mainly celebrated in which state?

  1. Punjab
  2. Gujarat
  3. Bihar
  4. Assam

Answer: A) Punjab

Lohri is a winter harvest festival mainly celebrated in Punjab and Haryana, observed on the night before Makar Sankranti around 13 January, with bonfires. It marks the harvest of rabi crops.

  1. Qutb-ud-din Aibak
  2. Iltutmish
  3. Alauddin Khalji
  4. Balban

Answer: A) Qutb-ud-din Aibak

Qutb-ud-din Aibak began Qutub Minar around 1199, and his successor Iltutmish completed it. Firoz Shah Tughlaq later repaired the upper storeys. It is in Delhi and a UNESCO site.

  1. West Bengal

9. Which of these is a percussion instrument?

  1. Sitar
  2. Shehnai
  3. Sarangi
  4. Mridangam

Answer: D) Mridangam

The mridangam is a double-headed percussion drum, central to Carnatic music in South India. The sitar, sarangi and shehnai are string and wind instruments. Tip: Mridangam = Carnatic drum.

17. Which classical dance is known for storytelling with spinning movements (chakkars) and is rooted in North India?

  1. Kathak
  2. Kathakali
  3. Kuchipudi
  4. Sattriya

Answer: A) Kathak

Kathak is the classical dance of North India, whose name comes from katha, meaning story. It features fast footwork and spinning chakkars, and developed Hindustani-style gharanas such as Jaipur and Lucknow.
