4. Consider the following statements: 1. Fiscal deficit equals total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings. 2. Revenue deficit equals revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts. 3. Primary deficit equals fiscal deficit plus interest payments. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. 1 and 2 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: C) 1 and 2 only

Statement 1 is correct: the fiscal deficit shows the government's total borrowing requirement. Statement 2 is correct: it measures the shortfall of revenue receipts against revenue expenditure. Statement 3 is wrong: primary deficit is fiscal deficit minus interest payments, showing borrowing needed for non-interest spending. So 1 and 2 only.

11. Consider the following statements: 1. The repo rate is the rate at which the RBI lends short-term funds to banks against government securities. 2. The reverse repo rate is the rate at which banks borrow from the RBI. 3. The Marginal Standing Facility rate is set above the repo rate. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 3 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: A) 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is correct: repo is collateralised lending by RBI. Statement 2 is wrong: under reverse repo the RBI borrows from banks, absorbing liquidity. Statement 3 is correct: the MSF rate sits 25 basis points above the repo rate and forms the upper limit of the policy corridor. So 1 and 3 only.

12. Which one of the following is NOT a function of the Reserve Bank of India?

  1. Acting as banker to the Government
  2. Acting as lender of last resort to banks
  3. Custodian of the country's foreign exchange reserves
  4. Issuing one-rupee currency notes

Answer: D) Issuing one-rupee currency notes

The RBI manages government accounts, supports banks as the lender of last resort and holds the foreign exchange reserves. However, one-rupee notes and all coins are issued by the Government of India through the Ministry of Finance, and the RBI only circulates them. The RBI issues notes of Rs 2 and above. So the last option is not an RBI function.

22. Which one of the following statements about national income concepts is NOT correct?

  1. GDP at market price equals GDP at factor cost plus indirect taxes minus subsidies
  2. GNP equals GDP plus net factor income from abroad
  3. Net Domestic Product equals GDP plus depreciation
  4. National income is NNP at factor cost

Answer: C) Net Domestic Product equals GDP plus depreciation

Market price GDP adds indirect taxes and subtracts subsidies to factor cost GDP, so the first is true. GNP adds net factor income from abroad to GDP, true. National income is conventionally NNP at factor cost, true. Net Domestic Product is GDP minus depreciation (capital consumption), so the third statement is incorrect.

28. Assertion (A): A rise in the repo rate tends to reduce demand-pull inflation. Reason (R): A higher repo rate makes bank borrowing costlier, which is passed on to customers and curbs credit-driven spending. Choose the correct option:

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

The Assertion is true: tightening monetary policy dampens aggregate demand and eases demand-pull price pressures. The Reason is also true and is the very mechanism: costlier funds for banks raise lending rates, discourage loans and reduce spending. Hence R correctly explains A.
