1. Consider the following statements about the Gupta period: 1. The Allahabad Pillar Inscription was composed by Harishena. 2. The Chinese pilgrim Fa-Hien visited India during the reign of Chandragupta II. 3. Harshavardhana, whose court Hiuen Tsang visited, belonged to the Gupta dynasty. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 3 only
  2. 2 only
  3. 1 and 2 only
  4. 1 only

Answer: C) 1 and 2 only

Statement 1 is true: It praises the conquests of Samudragupta. Statement 2 is true: He travelled in the early fifth century CE. Statement 3 is false: Harsha belonged to the Pushyabhuti dynasty.

2. Match the following works with their authors: A. Mudrarakshasa B. Rajatarangini C. Harshacharita D. Ashtadhyayi 1. Kalhana 2. Vishakhadatta 3. Banabhatta 4. Panini

  1. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
  2. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
  3. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  4. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4

Answer: D) A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4

Mudrarakshasa, the play on Chanakya and Chandragupta, was written by Vishakhadatta. Rajatarangini, the chronicle of Kashmir, was written by Kalhana. Harshacharita, a biography of Harsha, was written by Banabhatta. Ashtadhyayi, the Sanskrit grammar, is by Panini.

3. Consider the following statements about Ashoka and the Mauryan period: 1. Ashoka's conquest of Kalinga took place around 261 BCE. 2. James Prinsep deciphered the Brahmi script in 1837, which enabled reading of Ashokan inscriptions. 3. Ashoka appointed Dhamma Mahamattas to propagate Dhamma. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1, 2 and 3
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1 only

Answer: B) 1, 2 and 3

Statement 1 is true: Rock Edict XIII records the war and his remorse. Statement 2 is true: His work identified Devanampiya Piyadasi as Ashoka. Statement 3 is true: They were appointed around his thirteenth regnal year.

4. Which one of the following statements about the Cholas is NOT correct?

  1. The Uttaramerur inscriptions describe the working of village assemblies.
  2. Rajendra I founded Gangaikonda Cholapuram as a new capital.
  3. Vijayalaya Chola established Chola power by capturing Thanjavur.
  4. The Brihadisvara temple at Thanjavur was built by Rajendra I.

Answer: D) The Brihadisvara temple at Thanjavur was built by Rajendra I.

The Uttaramerur inscriptions record the election and functioning of village assemblies. Rajendra I founded Gangaikonda Cholapuram after his northern expedition. Vijayalaya captured Thanjavur in the mid-ninth century. The Brihadisvara temple was built by Rajaraja I, not Rajendra I.

14. Which is the oldest of the four Vedas?

  1. Samaveda
  2. Yajurveda
  3. Atharvaveda
  4. Rigveda

Answer: D) Rigveda

The Rigveda is the oldest of the four Vedas, composed around 1500-1000 BCE, with 10 mandalas and 1028 hymns. The other three are Samaveda, Yajurveda and Atharvaveda, which are later.

  2. Akbar

  2. Bronze
  3. Iron
