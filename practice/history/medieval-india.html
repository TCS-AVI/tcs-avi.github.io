1. Arrange the following battles in chronological order (earliest first): A. First Battle of Panipat B. Battle of Talikota C. First Battle of Tarain D. Battle of Khanwa

  1. C, D, A, B
  2. A, C, D, B
  3. C, A, D, B
  4. C, A, B, D

Answer: C) C, A, D, B

The First Battle of Tarain was fought in 1191. The First Battle of Panipat followed in 1526, then Khanwa in 1527, where Babur defeated Rana Sanga. The Battle of Talikota, which ended Vijayanagara power, was in 1565. The order is C, A, D, B.

  2. 1, 2 and 3
  3. 1 and 2 only

3. Match the foreign visitor with the ruler or kingdom they are associated with: A. Al-Biruni B. Ibn Battuta C. Abdur Razzaq D. Domingo Paes 1. Krishnadevaraya 2. Muhammad bin Tughlaq 3. Mahmud of Ghazni 4. Devaraya II

  1. A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
  2. A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1
  3. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
  4. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1

Answer: B) A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1

Al-Biruni accompanied Mahmud of Ghazni and wrote Kitab-ul-Hind. Ibn Battuta served as qazi under Muhammad bin Tughlaq. Abdur Razzaq visited Vijayanagara in the time of Devaraya II. Domingo Paes described Vijayanagara under Krishnadevaraya.

6. Which Mughal ruler built the Taj Mahal?

  1. Akbar
  2. Shah Jahan
  3. Jahangir
  4. Aurangzeb

Answer: B) Shah Jahan

7. Which Delhi Sultan introduced market control measures and price regulation?

  1. Alauddin Khalji
  2. Muhammad bin Tughlaq
  3. Firuz Shah Tughlaq
  4. Sikandar Lodi

Answer: A) Alauddin Khalji

Alauddin Khalji introduced market reforms with fixed prices of grain, cloth and horses, overseen by the Diwan-i-Riyasat and Shahna-i-Mandi, to support a large standing army. Tip: Alauddin equals price control.

8. Babur's autobiography, the Baburnama, was written in which language?

  1. Persian
  2. Urdu
  3. Chagatai Turkish
  4. Arabic

Answer: C) Chagatai Turkish

Babur wrote his memoirs, Baburnama (Tuzuk-i-Baburi), in Chagatai Turkish, his mother tongue. Abdur Rahim Khan-i-Khanan later translated it into Persian under Akbar.

  1. Ibrahim Lodi
  2. Rana Sanga

  4. Hemu

Answer: A) Ibrahim Lodi

11. Separate electorates for Muslims were introduced by which Act?

  1. Indian Councils Act, 1909 (Morley–Minto Reforms)
  2. Government of India Act, 1919
  3. Government of India Act, 1935
  4. Indian Councils Act, 1892

Answer: A) Indian Councils Act, 1909 (Morley–Minto Reforms)

The Indian Councils Act, 1909 (Morley-Minto Reforms) introduced separate electorates for Muslims, earning Minto the label 'father of communal electorate'. The 1919 Act extended separate electorates to Sikhs and others.

17. Which Mughal emperor founded the religion 'Din-i-Ilahi'?

  1. Akbar
  2. Jahangir
  3. Shah Jahan
  4. Aurangzeb

Answer: A) Akbar

Akbar promulgated Din-i-Ilahi (Tauhid-i-Ilahi) around 1582 as an ethical order blending ideas from many faiths, after discussions at the Ibadat Khana in Fatehpur Sikri. Birbal was its notable Hindu follower.

  1. Akbar
  2. Hemu
  3. Ibrahim Lodi

20. Who founded the Slave (Mamluk) dynasty of the Delhi Sultanate?

  1. Iltutmish
  2. Balban
  3. Qutbuddin Aibak
  4. Razia Sultana

Answer: C) Qutbuddin Aibak

Qutbuddin Aibak, a slave of Muhammad Ghori, founded the Slave or Mamluk dynasty in 1206 with capital at Lahore, later Delhi. He began the Qutub Minar and Quwwat-ul-Islam mosque. Tip: Aibak 1206.
