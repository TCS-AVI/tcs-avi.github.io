1. Consider the following statements about British constitutional acts in India: 1. The Regulating Act of 1773 abolished the Court of Directors. 2. Pitt's India Act of 1784 set up a Board of Control. 3. The Charter Act of 1833 made the Governor-General of Bengal the Governor-General of India. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: C) 2 and 3 only

Statement 1 is false: The Court of Directors continued; the Act only regulated the Company. Statement 2 is true: It supervised political affairs of the Company. Statement 3 is true: It also ended the Company's commercial functions.

3. Assertion (A): The Poona Pact of 1932 retained separate electorates for the Depressed Classes. Reason (R): It increased the reserved seats for the Depressed Classes in provincial legislatures compared with the Communal Award. Select the correct answer:

  1. A is false but R is true
  2. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  3. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  4. A is true but R is false

Answer: A) A is false but R is true

A is false: the Poona Pact replaced separate electorates with reserved seats within general electorates, ending Gandhi's fast. R is true: reserved seats rose from 71 under the Communal Award to 148 under the Pact.

5. Assertion (A): Mahatma Gandhi suspended the Non-Cooperation Movement in February 1922. Reason (R): A mob set fire to a police station at Chauri Chaura, killing policemen. Select the correct answer:

  1. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  2. A is true but R is false
  3. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: C) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

A is true: Gandhi called off the movement after the Chauri Chaura incident of 5 February 1922. R is also true, as a mob burnt the police station and killed policemen. Gandhi withdrew the movement because of this violence, so R explains A.

8. Which one of the following statements about the Government of India Act, 1919 is NOT correct?

  1. It introduced dyarchy in the provinces.
  2. It established the Federal Court of India.
  3. It provided for a bicameral Central Legislature.
  4. It extended separate electorates to Sikhs, Anglo-Indians, Indian Christians and Europeans.

Answer: B) It established the Federal Court of India.

The 1919 Act introduced dyarchy in the provinces and created a bicameral Central Legislature, with a Council of State and a Legislative Assembly. It extended communal electorates to more communities. The Federal Court was set up under the Government of India Act, 1935, so that statement is incorrect.

9. Arrange the following in chronological order (earliest first): A. Lucknow Pact B. Partition of Bengal C. Jallianwala Bagh massacre D. Formation of the All India Muslim League

  1. B, D, A, C
  2. D, B, A, C
  3. B, A, D, C
  4. B, D, C, A

Answer: A) B, D, A, C

The Partition of Bengal took effect in 1905. The All India Muslim League was formed in Dhaka in 1906. The Lucknow Pact between the Congress and the League was signed in 1916. The Jallianwala Bagh massacre occurred in 1919. The order is B, D, A, C.

10. Arrange the following in chronological order (earliest first): A. Battle of Buxar B. Battle of Plassey C. Treaty of Allahabad D. Battle of Wandiwash

  1. B, A, D, C
  2. D, B, A, C
  3. B, D, C, A
  4. B, D, A, C

Answer: D) B, D, A, C

Plassey was fought in 1757. Wandiwash, where the British defeated the French, was in 1760. Buxar followed in 1764. The Treaty of Allahabad, granting the Diwani of Bengal, came in 1765. The order is B, D, A, C.
