1. Assertion (A): Ladakh Union Territory does not have a legislature. Reason (R): Leh and Kargil have Autonomous Hill Development Councils. Select the correct answer.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A

Assertion is true: the Reorganisation Act created Ladakh as a UT without legislature. Reason is true: the Ladakh Autonomous Hill Development Councils exist for Leh and Kargil. The councils are local bodies and are not the cause of the absence of a legislature, so R does not explain A.

2. Consider the following statements about the Union Territories created in 2019: 1. Jammu and Kashmir UT has a legislature, while Ladakh UT does not. 2. Ladakh UT comprised the districts of Leh and Kargil at its formation. 3. Ladakh UT has a Legislative Assembly of 30 elected members. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 only
  3. 2 only
  4. 1 and 3 only

Answer: A) 1 and 2 only

The Reorganisation Act provided a legislature for Jammu and Kashmir, whereas Ladakh was created as a UT without legislature, so statement 1 is true. Ladakh began with Leh and Kargil districts, so statement 2 is true. Statement 3 is false because Ladakh has no Assembly.

3. Assertion (A): Gulab Singh became Maharaja of Jammu and Kashmir through the Treaty of Amritsar. Reason (R): By the Treaty of Lahore of 9 March 1846 the Lahore Darbar had ceded Kashmir and nearby hill territories to the British in lieu of war indemnity. Select the correct answer.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Assertion is true: the Treaty of Amritsar (16 March 1846) made Gulab Singh ruler. Reason is true: after the First Anglo-Sikh War the Treaty of Lahore ceded the hill territories between the Beas and Indus, including Kashmir, in part payment of indemnity. Because the British held Kashmir, they could sell it to Gulab Singh, so R explains A.
