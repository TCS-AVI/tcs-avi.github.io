1. Match the following lakes with their features: A. Wular B. Sambhar C. Pulicat D. Loktak 1. Inland salt lake of Rajasthan 2. Floating biomass called phumdis, Manipur 3. Freshwater lake of tectonic origin in Jammu and Kashmir 4. Lagoon on the Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu coast

  1. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2
  2. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
  3. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2
  4. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2

Answer: D) A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2

Wular in Kashmir is a large freshwater lake of tectonic origin. Sambhar is India's largest inland salt lake in Rajasthan. Pulicat is a coastal lagoon shared by Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu, separated from the sea by Sriharikota island. Loktak in Manipur is known for its floating phumdis. So A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2.

7. Consider the following statements: 1. Black soil is formed by weathering of basaltic rocks and is rich in lime, iron, magnesium and alumina. 2. Laterite soil is rich in nitrogen and humus because of heavy leaching. 3. Khadar is the newer alluvium of river floodplains and is more fertile than Bhangar. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. All of the above

Answer: C) 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is correct: regur soil derives from Deccan basalt and has these minerals. Statement 2 is wrong: intense leaching in laterite removes nutrients, leaving it poor in nitrogen, humus and lime. Statement 3 is correct: Khadar is renewed by annual floods and is finer and more fertile than the older, kankar-bearing Bhangar. Hence 1 and 3 only.

9. Assertion (A): The Thar desert receives scanty rainfall from the southwest monsoon. Reason (R): The Arabian Sea branch of the monsoon blows parallel to the Aravalli range and meets no barrier to force the moist air up. Choose the correct option:

  1. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: B) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

The Assertion is true: western Rajasthan lies in a rain shadow and gets very little monsoon rain. The Reason is also true: the Arabian Sea branch runs along the line of the Aravallis, so little orographic lifting occurs. Because the winds are not forced to rise and cool, they yield little rain, so R explains A.

10. Which one of the following statements about the Himalayas is NOT correct?

  1. Karewas are lacustrine deposits found in the Kashmir valley
  2. The Duns, such as Dehradun, lie between the Lesser Himalaya and the Shiwaliks
  3. The Shiwalik range is the highest range of the Himalayas
  4. The Siachen glacier lies in the Karakoram range

Answer: C) The Shiwalik range is the highest range of the Himalayas

Karewas are lake-laid deposits of the Kashmir valley used for saffron and orchards, so the first statement is true. Duns are longitudinal valleys between Lesser Himalaya and Shiwaliks, also true. The Siachen glacier is in the Karakoram, true. The Shiwaliks are the outermost and lowest range; the Himadri is the highest, so the third statement is incorrect.

18. Assertion (A): Black soil retains moisture for a long time. Reason (R): Black soil contains a very high proportion of sand and very little clay. Choose the correct option:

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: C) A is true but R is false

The Assertion is true: regur soil swells when wet and cracks when dry and holds moisture well, which suits cotton. The Reason is false: black soil is clayey, with fine particles, not sandy. It is the high clay content that gives its high water-holding capacity.
