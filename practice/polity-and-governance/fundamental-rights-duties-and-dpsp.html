1. Assertion (A): Article 19 freedoms are automatically suspended whenever a National Emergency is declared, on any ground. Reason (R): The 44th Amendment replaced 'internal disturbance' with 'armed rebellion' as a ground for Emergency. Select the correct answer:

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: D) A is false but R is true

A is false: under Article 358 as amended by the 44th Amendment, Article 19 is suspended only when Emergency is declared on grounds of war or external aggression, not armed rebellion. R is true, as the 44th Amendment made this substitution in Article 352.

2. Which one of the following is NOT a Directive Principle of State Policy?

  1. Equality before law and equal protection of laws (Article 14)
  2. Uniform civil code for citizens (Article 44)
  3. Organisation of village panchayats (Article 40)
  4. Separation of judiciary from executive in public services (Article 50)

Answer: A) Equality before law and equal protection of laws (Article 14)

Articles 36 to 51 in Part IV contain the Directive Principles. Article 40 on village panchayats, Article 44 on a uniform civil code and Article 50 on separating the judiciary from the executive are all in Part IV. Article 14 is a Fundamental Right in Part III.

3. Consider the following statements about the Election Commission of India: 1. Article 324 vests the superintendence, direction and control of elections to Parliament and State Legislatures in the Election Commission. 2. The Chief Election Commissioner can be removed in like manner and on like grounds as a Judge of the Supreme Court. 3. The Election Commission of India conducts elections to Panchayats and Municipalities. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 1 only
  3. 3 only
  4. 1 and 3 only

Answer: A) 1 and 2 only

Statement 1 is true: Article 324 gives the Commission this authority. Statement 2 is true: Article 324(5) proviso grants this security. Statement 3 is false: State Election Commissions do this under Articles 243K and 243ZA.

5. Consider the following statements about the 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992: 1. It inserted Part IXA in the Constitution dealing with Municipalities. 2. 6. Consider the following statements about the judiciary: 1. Under Article 226 High Courts can issue writs for enforcing fundamental rights and for any other purpose. 2. A Judge of the Supreme Court holds office until the age of 65 years. 3. The jurisdiction of the Supreme Court can be enlarged only by a Presidential order. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 2 and 3 only
  2. 1, 2 and 3
  3. 2 only
  4. 1 and 2 only

Answer: D) 1 and 2 only

Statement 1 is true: This makes Article 226 wider than Article 32. Statement 2 is true: Article 124(2) fixes the age at 65. Statement 3 is false: Parliament may enlarge it by law under Article 138.

7. Match the following: A. Article 76 B. Article 148 C. Article 280 D. Article 315 1. Finance Commission 2. Public Service Commissions 3. Attorney General of India 4. Comptroller and Auditor General

  1. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2
  2. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2
  3. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1
  4. A-1, B-4, C-3, D-2

Answer: A) A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

Article 76 provides for the Attorney General of India. Article 148 provides for the Comptroller and Auditor General. Article 280 constitutes the Finance Commission. Article 315 establishes Public Service Commissions for the Union and States. So A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2 is correct.

8. Match the Constitutional Amendment with its subject: A. 42nd Amendment B. 44th Amendment C. 61st Amendment D. 101st Amendment 1. Goods and Services Tax 2. Voting age lowered to 18 years 3. Added 'socialist' and 'secular' to the Preamble 4. Removed right to property as a Fundamental Right

  1. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
  2. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2
  3. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1
  4. A-3, B-2, C-4, D-1

Answer: C) A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1

The 42nd Amendment (1976) added socialist, secular and integrity to the Preamble. The 44th (1978) removed property from Fundamental Rights. The 61st (1988) reduced the voting age from 21 to 18. The 101st (2016) introduced GST. Hence A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1.

13. The writ of Mandamus means:

  1. We command
  2. By what authority
  3. To be certified
  4. To forbid

Answer: A) We command

Mandamus is Latin for 'we command'. A court issues it to direct a public official, body or lower court to perform a legal duty it has failed to do. It does not lie against the President or Governor, or private individuals.

  2. Part IV
  3. Part V
  4. Part III

  1. Part IV
  2. Part III
  3. Part IV-A

24. Which writ is issued to free a person from illegal detention?

  1. Mandamus
  2. Certiorari
  3. Quo Warranto
  4. Habeas Corpus

Answer: D) Habeas Corpus

Habeas corpus, Latin for 'you may have the body', directs authorities to produce a detained person before court and justify the detention. It protects personal liberty and can be issued by the Supreme Court (Article 32) and High Courts (Article 226).
