1. Consider the following statements about the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019: 1. It created the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir with a legislature and the Union Territory of Ladakh without a legislature. 2. The Act came into force on 31 October 2019. 3. A common High Court serves both Union Territories. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 and 3 only
  2. 3 only
  3. 1 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: D) 1, 2 and 3

Statement 1 is true: This is the Act's basic arrangement. Statement 2 is true: That date was notified as the appointed day. Statement 3 is true: The High Court of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh serves both.

2. Consider the following statements about the National Human Rights Commission (NHRC): 1. It is a constitutional body established under Article 338. 2. Its Chairperson is appointed by the President on the recommendation of a committee headed by the Prime Minister. 3. Its recommendations are binding on the government concerned. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 2 only
  2. 1, 2 and 3
  3. 1 and 2 only
  4. 2 and 3 only

Answer: A) 2 only

Statement 1 is false: It is a statutory body under the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993. Statement 2 is true: The Act prescribes this selection committee. Statement 3 is false: They are recommendatory; the authority must only report action taken.

3. Consider the following statements about the Tenth Schedule (anti-defection law): 1. It was added to the Constitution by the 52nd Amendment in 1985. 2. The 91st Amendment retained the exemption for a split of one-third of a legislature party. 3. In Kihoto Hollohan the Supreme Court held the presiding officer's decision subject to limited judicial review. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1, 2 and 3
  2. 1 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 2 only

Answer: C) 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is true: The 52nd Amendment inserted the Tenth Schedule. Statement 2 is false: The 91st Amendment, 2003, deleted the split exemption. Statement 3 is true: The Court struck down the paragraph 7 bar on review.

5. What is the retirement age of a Supreme Court judge?

  1. 60 years
  2. 62 years
  3. 70 years
  4. 65 years

Answer: D) 65 years

Supreme Court judges retire at 65 under Article 124, while High Court judges retire at 62 under Article 217. Tip: SC 65, HC 62. A judge can resign earlier by writing to the President.
