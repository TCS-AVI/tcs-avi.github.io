1. Assertion (A): The Rajya Sabha is a permanent House not subject to dissolution. Reason (R): One-third of its members retire every second year. Select the correct answer:

  1. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Answer: B) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

A is true because Article 83(1) says the Rajya Sabha is not subject to dissolution. R is also true, since one-third retire every second year under Article 83(1) read with the law. The staggered retirement keeps the House continuous, so R explains A.

2. Consider the following statements regarding Money Bills: 1. A Money Bill can be introduced in either House of Parliament. 2. The Rajya Sabha must return a Money Bill to the Lok Sabha within 14 days of receipt. 3. The decision of the Speaker on whether a Bill is a Money Bill is final. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 only
  4. 3 only

Answer: B) 2 and 3 only

Statement 1 is false: Article 109 permits introduction only in the Lok Sabha. Statement 2 is true: Article 109(2) sets this limit. Statement 3 is true: Article 110(3) makes the Speaker's certificate final.

3. Consider the following statements about amendment of the Constitution: 1. Under Article 368, a Bill needs a majority of the total membership and two-thirds of members present and voting in each House. 2. Every Constitution amendment Bill requires ratification by the legislatures of at least half of the States. 3. The basic structure doctrine was propounded in the Kesavananda Bharati case of 1973. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: C) 1 and 3 only

Statement 1 is true: This is the special majority for most amendments. Statement 2 is false: Only federal provisions listed in Article 368(2) need ratification. Statement 3 is true: The Court held Parliament cannot alter the basic structure.

4. Consider the following statements about impeachment of the President under Article 61: 1. It can be initiated only in the Lok Sabha. 2. The resolution requires fourteen days' notice signed by at least one-fourth of the members of the House. 3. Nominated members of Parliament can participate in the impeachment. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1, 2 and 3
  3. 2 only
  4. 2 and 3 only

Answer: D) 2 and 3 only

Statement 1 is false: Either House of Parliament may initiate it. Statement 2 is true: Article 61(2) requires this notice. Statement 3 is true: They are barred only from voting in the Presidential election.

5. Which one of the following statements about the Vice-President of India is NOT correct?

  1. He is ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
  2. He must be qualified for election as a member of the Rajya Sabha.
  3. Elected members of State Legislative Assemblies are part of the electoral college that elects him.
  4. He is elected by an electoral college consisting of members of both Houses of Parliament.

Answer: C) Elected members of State Legislative Assemblies are part of the electoral college that elects him.

Article 63 and 64 make the Vice-President ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha. Article 66(3) requires qualification for membership of the Rajya Sabha. Article 66(1) provides that the electoral college consists of members of both Houses of Parliament. State Assembly members do not vote, so that statement is incorrect.

6. Consider the following statements about the Governor of a State: 1. The Governor is elected by the members of the State Legislative Assembly. 2. The Governor can promulgate an ordinance even while the Legislative Assembly is in session. 3. The Governor is an integral part of the State Legislature. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 3 only
  2. 1 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 2 only
  4. 2 and 3 only

Answer: A) 3 only

Statement 1 is false: The President appoints the Governor under Article 155. Statement 2 is false: Article 213 permits ordinances only when the Legislature is not in session. Statement 3 is true: Article 168 includes the Governor in the Legislature.
