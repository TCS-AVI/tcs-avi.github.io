1. Consider the following statements about the Jammu and Kashmir Panchayati Raj Act, 1989: 1. It provided for a three-tier structure of Panchayati Raj in the state. 2. The Block Development Council was the lowest tier under the Act. 3. The apex tier under the Act was called the Zila Parishad. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 1 and 2 only
  3. 2 and 3 only
  4. 3 only

Answer: A) 1 only

Statement 1 is true: The tiers were Halqa Panchayat, Block Development Council and District Planning and Development Board. Statement 2 is false: The village-level Halqa Panchayat was the lowest tier. Statement 3 is false: The apex tier was the District Planning and Development Board.

2. Arrange the following in chronological order (earliest first): A. 42nd Constitutional Amendment B. 86th Constitutional Amendment C. First Constitutional Amendment D. Kesavananda Bharati judgment

  1. C, A, D, B
  2. C, D, A, B
  3. D, C, A, B
  4. C, D, B, A

Answer: B) C, D, A, B

The First Amendment came in 1951. The Kesavananda Bharati judgment was delivered in 1973. The 42nd Amendment followed in 1976. The 86th Amendment, inserting Article 21A, was enacted in 2002. The order is C, D, A, B.
