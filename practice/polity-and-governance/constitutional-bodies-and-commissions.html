1. Consider the following statements about the 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992: 1. The Eleventh Schedule lists 18 subjects for Panchayats. 2. It inserted Part IX in the Constitution, comprising Articles 243 to 243O. 3. Seats in Panchayats are reserved for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in proportion to their population. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 3 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1, 2 and 3
  4. 2 only

Answer: B) 2 and 3 only

Statement 1 is false: It lists 29 subjects; 18 items belong to the Twelfth Schedule. Statement 2 is true: Part IX is titled The Panchayats. Statement 3 is true: Article 243D provides proportional reservation.
