1. Activated charcoal is NOT effective in adsorbing

  1. Paracetamol
  2. Aspirin
  3. Iron salts
  4. Phenobarbital

Answer: C) Iron salts

Iron, lithium, alcohols and strong acids/alkalis are poorly adsorbed by activated charcoal.

10. Consider the statements: 1. Propranolol can precipitate bronchospasm in asthmatic patients. 2. Atenolol is a non-selective beta blocker. 3. Beta blockers may mask the adrenergic warning symptoms of hypoglycaemia. Which are correct?

  1. 1 and 2 only
  2. 2 and 3 only
  3. 1 and 3 only
  4. 1, 2 and 3

Answer: C) 1 and 3 only

Non-selective beta blockade blocks bronchial beta-2 receptors, and beta blockers mask tachycardia and tremor of hypoglycaemia. Atenolol is beta-1 selective.

30. Match the drug (Column I) with its mechanism (Column II): A. Dantrolene B. Baclofen C. Succinylcholine D. Tizanidine 1. Agonist at GABA-B receptors 2. Blocks ryanodine receptor calcium release in muscle 3. Persistent depolarisation of the motor end plate 4. Central alpha-2 agonist

  1. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4

  3. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3

37. Arrange the biosynthesis of catecholamines in the correct sequence:

  1. Tyrosine, dopamine, L-DOPA, noradrenaline, adrenaline
  2. Tyrosine, L-DOPA, dopamine, noradrenaline, adrenaline
  3. Tyrosine, L-DOPA, noradrenaline, dopamine, adrenaline
  4. Tyrosine, L-DOPA, dopamine, adrenaline, noradrenaline

Answer: B) Tyrosine, L-DOPA, dopamine, noradrenaline, adrenaline

Sequence: tyrosine (tyrosine hydroxylase) to L-DOPA, (DOPA decarboxylase) to dopamine, (dopamine beta-hydroxylase) to noradrenaline, (PNMT) to adrenaline.

39. Assertion (A): Dimercaprol is the antidote of choice in iron poisoning. Reason (R): Dimercaprol contains two sulfhydryl groups that chelate heavy metals.

  1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
  2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
  3. A is true but R is false
  4. A is false but R is true

Dimercaprol chelates arsenic, mercury and gold through sulfhydryl groups; iron is treated with deferoxamine.
