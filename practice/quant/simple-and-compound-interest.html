1. What is the compound interest (in Rs) on Rs 10000 at 10% per annum for 2 years (compounded annually)?

  1. 1925
  2. 2625
  3. 2100
  4. 2275

Answer: C) 2100

A = 10000 x (1+10/100)^2 = 12100; CI = 12100 - 10000 = 2100.
