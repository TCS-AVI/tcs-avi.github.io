UPGRADE THE LIVE SITE (v3)
==========================
Repo: tcs-avi.github.io

A. Upload files (Add file > Upload files, then Commit changes)
   index.html, sw.js, review.html, reviewed.js, build_pages.py, update.py, robots.txt, sitemap.xml
   DO NOT upload data.js or bank.js (data.js holds the bot's latest work; bank.js has not changed).

B. Replace the workflow
   Open .github/workflows/update.yml, click the pencil, replace everything with the text of update.yml.txt, commit.

C. Build the new pages now
   Actions > Daily update > Run workflow. Wait for the green tick (about 1 minute).
   The folders practice/, exams/ and share/ then appear in the repo.

D. Check
   https://tcs-avi.github.io/practice/   https://tcs-avi.github.io/exams/
   https://tcs-avi.github.io/share/      https://tcs-avi.github.io/review.html

E. Tell Google (free, optional but recommended)
   Go to search.google.com/search-console, add https://tcs-avi.github.io/ and submit sitemap.xml.
